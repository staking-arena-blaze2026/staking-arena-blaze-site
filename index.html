Stake Arena is a competitive market where users compete to lock up BCH or CashTokens in exchange for rewards.

This is not only a standalone product, but also a component to a novel stablecoin design. You can check out another component here.

How It Works

Creators deploy vaults with a BCH or CashTokens reward for distribution to those who want it the most.

Stakers pick a vault and make a bid to lock the required asset in exchange for a reward. They decide how much they want to lock, for how long and how much of the reward they want. They are queued based on their score:

Priority Score = (Stake Amount × Lock Duration) / Requested Reward.

You can find more information about how it works and its use cases in the About section.
